**Reading the table.** TUFS is a small, hand-built basic vocabulary, so
it measures coverage of everyday words better than it finds errors.
Spot checks of the disagreements:

* *Word only in other synsets* is mostly a missing sense in our wordnet
  (学习 'study', *menulis* 'write' in the 'write down' synset), not a
  wrong one.
* *No word in common* is mostly synonyms (TUFS 房子, ours 住宅) or
  TUFS conventions: Japanese verbs in the polite form (始まります for
  始まる) and words under another part of speech (嫌い for the verb
  'dislike').  Some are real errors in ours from English ambiguity,
  e.g. *memperhatikan* 'notice' in the Indonesian 'note, write down'
  synset.
* Japanese matches use all written forms (kana spellings included);
  TUFS often writes in kana or with お.
